Hydroponic System Types
💧DWCEASY

Deep Water Culture — roots suspended in oxygenated solution. Air pump 24/7. Most popular beginner system.

✦ Low cost   ✦ Fast growth   ✦ Best for lettuce
🌊NFTEASY

Nutrient Film Technique — thin film flows over roots in sloped channels. Excellent efficiency and scalability.

✦ Water-efficient   ✦ Scalable   ✦ Commercial choice
🌫️AeroponicsADVANCED

Roots misted every 1–5 min in air chamber. Maximum O₂ delivers fastest growth rates known. NASA technology.

✦ Fastest growth   ✦ Space farm tech
🪣Ebb & FlowMEDIUM

Grow bed flooded periodically then drains back to reservoir by gravity. Timer-controlled pump cycles.

✦ Versatile   ✦ Good for fruiting ✦ Easy to automate
🎋KratkyEASY

Fully passive — no pump. Roots bridge nutrient-air gap naturally as level drops. Off-grid capable.

✦ No power   ✦ Set and forget   ✦ Herbs & greens
🌱Drip SystemMEDIUM

Nutrient solution drips via emitters to plant base. Standard in commercial greenhouses. Highly scalable.

✦ Any crop   ✦ Scalable   ✦ Precise control

Essential Nutrients — What Plants Eat
🍃 Nitrogen
N — NO₃⁻ / NH₄⁺
Drives leaf and stem growth. Essential for chlorophyll synthesis. Deficiency causes yellowing of older leaves.
High
🌸 Phosphorus
P — H₂PO₄⁻
Root development and flowering. ATP energy transfer. Low P causes dark purple discolouration.
Med
💪 Potassium
K — K⁺
Stomata regulation, water uptake, disease resistance. Deficiency = brown scorched leaf margins.
Med
🦴 Calcium
Ca — Ca²⁺
Cell wall structural integrity. Prevents blossom end rot in tomatoes. Critical during fruiting stage.
Med
🌀 Magnesium
Mg — Mg²⁺
Central atom of chlorophyll molecule. Backbone of photosynthesis. Deficiency = interveinal chlorosis.
OK
⚡ Iron
Fe — Fe²⁺/Fe³⁺
Chlorophyll synthesis enzyme cofactor. Must use chelated form (EDTA/DTPA) to maintain solubility at pH >6.
Low

IoT Automation Logic — Step by Step
INPUT
🔬 Sensors Collect Continuous Data
pH probe, EC meter, temp sensors, DO sensor, flow meter and light sensors all sample at configured intervals (5s–5min). Digitised over I²C / UART / analog GPIO to microcontroller firmware.
↓
PROCESS
🧠 PID Controller Evaluates Thresholds
ESP32/RPi compares every reading against programmed setpoints. Decision tree running at 1Hz: pH > 6.5 → dose acid · EC < 1.2 → dose nutrients · Temp > 28°C → start fan + chiller · DO < 6.0 → boost air pump
↓
ACTUATE
💧 pH Correction
Peristaltic pump doses pH-Up (KOH) or pH-Down (H₃PO₄). System waits 60s for mixing, then re-checks before further dosing.
ACTUATE
🧪 Nutrient Dosing
Part A (Ca, K) then Part B (Mg, micronutrients) dosed sequentially with mixing delay to prevent precipitation reactions.
ACTUATE
💡 Light Schedule
Relay switches LED grow lights per precise photoperiod. 18h/6h veg · 12h/12h flower. Dimming via PWM for dawn/dusk simulation.
↓
TRANSMIT
📡 MQTT Data Stream → Cloud
Sensor readings published every 30 seconds. Topics: farm/ph · farm/ec · farm/temp · farm/do · farm/ppfd. Broker relays to Thingsboard/Firebase for persistent storage and live visualisation.
↓
ALERT
🚨 Anomaly Detection & Failsafe
pH < 4.5 or > 8.0 → Emergency pump shutdown + Telegram alert + SMS + email. All critical events logged with ISO timestamp for audit trail.
OUTPUT
📱 Remote Dashboard & Override
Grower views live charts and historical trends from anywhere via mobile app. Manual override of any actuator in real time. Camera feed optional.
